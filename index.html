About

Pirates Bloodline is a fusion of cultures and hypnotic grooves — a DJ, curator, and sonic storyteller rooted in Moroccan Amazigh heritage and the maritime memory of the Atlantic coast.

Drawing from the far reaches of the East to the Berber shoreline, his sets weave mesmerizing beats, pulsing basslines, and hypnotic melodies into an ever-evolving soundscape. From the dark dancefloors of Fusion Festival and Kater Blau to intimate stages across Europe — each performance becomes a vessel carrying ancestral echoes and the rebellious spirit of dance.

"Beyond music, Pirates Bloodline weaves narrative and myth into each performance, turning the dancefloor into a shared voyage."

As founder of Circie Records and with releases on Proton Music, the project extends beyond performance into production, curation, and cultural storytelling.
